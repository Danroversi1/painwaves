    Relief comes from two clinical trials. Curves are fitted to the posted results of a dental-surgery trial (single dose; combination, naproxen, acetaminophen, placebo) and a bunion-surgery trial (every 12 hours for 48 hours). Pick an arm under Relief curve; placebo shows how little relief happens without a drug. Details on the Science board.

    Five sound personas

    Built from the 600 answers to “how would you describe your pain if it was a noise you can't escape?”. Choose one to play it on the stage, at any age.

    1 · Code every answer

    Each answer is tagged for time pattern (constant, pulsing, waves, sudden), timbre (impact, buzz, ringing, drone…), pitch, loudness and body cues, using transparent keyword rules.

    2 · Pair time with timbre

    A persona is a pairing that recurs: the answers where a time pattern and a timbre turn up together most often, plus the body cue that comes with them. Rules are applied in order; each answer joins one persona at most.

    3 · Add age as a modifier

    The persona sets the core sound. Age adjusts it using shifts measured across all 600 people (pitch, buzz, constancy, pulse), because single persona-by-age groups are too small to stand alone.
