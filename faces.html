Load a face. Watch it carry the pain.

A face is read into about 480 points, rebuilt as a field of particles with its own depth, and then driven by the same numbers as the other boards: a pain profile from 678 people and a clinical relief curve. The expression follows the four facial actions that pain research scores: brow lowering, tightening around the eyes, nose and upper-lip raising, and eye closure. Everything runs on this device, in the browser.

Where the face comes from. Two small models (MediaPipe Face Landmarker and Image Segmenter, downloaded once from Google and jsDelivr) find the points of the face and separate the person from the background; the photo itself is never uploaded. The pain level and relief curve are the same ones used on the Creative board. The expression is built from the published pain-face formula, so it shows how pain is scored on a face, not how this particular person would look.
